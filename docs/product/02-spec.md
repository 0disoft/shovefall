## Product

바닥이 사라지는 술래잡기 (`Shovefall`) is a single-player browser party-action game with no public round time limit; play continues until one participant remains or nobody survives. One human and deterministic rule-based bots shove each other from a collapsing tile arena. The game must explain itself through movement, telegraphs, impacts, and falling rather than a long tutorial or decorative marketing copy. A target human-play duration distribution remains a playtest decision rather than a claim inferred from bot-only rounds.

The central promise is a readable comic reversal: a player can dodge an incoming shove so that the attacker stumbles into the void, or two attackers can collide on the same tick and both fly away. A result may be chaotic, but it must follow visible state and deterministic rules rather than a hidden probability roll.

## Core Loop

1. The first visit has no selected starting attributes, skills, or item. `게임 시작` opens a setup-required dialog until the human saves exactly 20 attribute points, two unique skills, and one item; afterward it begins with those last saved in-memory settings. Settings and the concise version history remain secondary menu branches.
2. Before the round, the human allocates exactly 20 points across six starting attributes, selects two unique reusable skills, and chooses one charged item. Movement uses arrow keys, a right-clicked supported-ground destination, a touch joystick, a standard gamepad stick, or its D-pad. Desktop left-drag never acts as a joystick. A new right-click replaces destination travel, while direct movement input cancels it. `Q/W` select the two skills, `E` selects the shared Grappling Hook, and `D` selects the single inventory slot; pressing an action key enters a visible aim preview instead of casting immediately. While aiming, arrows move the target instead of the participant. Pressing the same action key or `Enter` confirms, while pointer users retain hover plus left-click confirmation. Ground actions walk into range before placement, a new movement input cancels that approach, invalid water targets remain unconfirmed, and `Escape` or right-click cancels. Self-target actions execute without an unnecessary aim step. `WASD`, `Space`, and `Shift` are unbound. `P` toggles a manual pause layer containing live round statistics and non-combat actions. A full `D` slot rejects map pickups until depleted. A credited elimination pauses the round and offers Strength, Agility, Constitution, Spirit, Balance, and Willpower as six direct trait choices. Each reward level grants exactly the same linear increment as one matching pre-round point; the legacy progression IDs remain serialization details only. Every uncapped trait is available without prerequisites, and skill ranks are not offered as kill rewards. A valid saved choice resumes play, while elimination and round completion take priority.
   Every participant begins with 30 current mana regardless of maximum-mana bonuses. Maximum mana and regeneration still derive from Spirit and later progression. The menu places a `소스 코드` link directly below `버전 기록`; it opens the public `https://github.com/0disoft/shovefall` repository in a new tab.
3. Ordinary locomotion starts, turns, and stops immediately at the current mass-sensitive top speed. Acceleration and retained momentum apply only to external launch, stumble, dodge, Grappling Hook, and falling states; no hidden probability decides an outcome.
4. Eight starting items are already present when play begins. Two treasure ships orbit opposite sides of the island and alternate the configured seven-second cycle, launching one visible gift about every 3.5 seconds when capacity permits. A target must be stable land three to seven tiles inward from its nearest water boundary at launch. The item does not exist until the gift lands seventy-two ticks later, and each ship keeps at most one gift airborne.
5. Each of the sixteen pirate ships owns an independent seeded firing clock and attempts one currently exposed outer-coast shot every 1.5 to 2.25 seconds. A shot enters the round only when that ship can complete an unobstructed full flight; its target tile shows the orange exclamation on the exact launch tick, then changes to the red skull stage before only that one warned tile turns to water. Reserved targets cannot receive duplicate warnings. Each actual impact extends the outer-ocean frontier for later targets, so an airborne projectile never exposes deeper land early. Ships sit 5.25 tiles offshore along the local outer-water direction, leaving roughly 3-4 visible water tiles between hull and shore. Ships have no ammunition counter or terminal rock phase: cannon fire continues until no stable land remains.
6. The last active participant wins. A defeated human can restart immediately or watch no more than five seconds of accelerated resolution.

## Player and Mode Contract

- Simulation and replay support: 4 through 70 participants, including the human; counts below 70 remain internal fixtures and focused diagnostic scenarios.
- Browser mode: exactly 70 participants with Hard AI.
- Starting build: exactly 20 integer points across Strength, Agility, Constitution, Spirit, Balance, and Willpower; each attribute is bounded to `0..20`, so a valid build may invest all points in one attribute. Every point has the same linear value with no soft cap or hidden neutral threshold. One Strength point grants 2.5% mass and 3.75% outgoing force. One Agility point grants 3.5% direct movement, 4% cooldown reduction, 2% skill-mana-cost reduction, and 2.5% shorter stumble. One Constitution point grants 1.75 maximum health and 1.25% health regeneration. One Spirit point grants 8 maximum mana, 10% mana regeneration, and 2% skill damage. One Balance point grants 5% incoming-impulse resistance and 4% control-duration reduction. One Willpower point grants 1.25% health-damage reduction and 1.25% shield strength. A kill-reward level applies the identical matching increment and reads these coefficients rather than copying a second balance table.
- Arena policy: a 57×48 procedural-island bound with exactly 12 separated 5–9-tile lakes under a 96-tile total budget for the public mode. The rectangular bound is 19.6% larger than the former 52×44 map, and presentation applies an 8% camera zoom without changing world-space input or collision coordinates.
- Public pacing: fixed Slow collapse, 8 pre-delivered initial map items, and two opposite treasure ships. Each ship launches one new item every 7 seconds when capacity permits; their clocks are offset, so the arena receives one gift about every 3.5 seconds. Gift targets stay 3–7 tiles inland at launch, flight lasts 72 ticks before collection, and the public round has no time limit.
- Arena area, spawn spacing, item caps, and bot search bounds are derived from participant count and density rather than exposed as unrelated raw numbers.

## Gray-box Gate

The current gate uses flat shapes and no final art. It must prove movement, weak contact, shove windup/active/recovery, dodge, missed-shove momentum, simultaneous shove resolution, two mass extremes, support loss, falling, and restart with two to eight participants.

Collapse, formal bots, 32-participant scale, items, audio, and final art cannot be used to hide a failed core interaction. They are promoted only after the gray-box interactions are deterministic, readable, and covered by scenario tests.

## MVP Scope

- A menu-first game start plus bounded settings for an exact 20-point starting build, two selected skills, and one starting item; participant count, bot difficulty, collapse speed, initial map-item count, respawn interval, and round limit are fixed rather than exposed.
- A concise in-game version history that records why each important product change was made and what players can notice.
- One human and 69 bots using the same `ActorCommandV1` contract.
- A 60 Hz fixed-tick simulation independent of PixiJS and browser time.
- Deterministic immediate locomotion, eight selectable reusable skills, one charged-item slot, one built-in Grappling Hook, stumble, mass, collision, support, falling, elimination credit, and kill-triggered direct trait progression.
- Deterministic health and mana resources, delayed health regeneration, continuous mana regeneration, shields, damage, stun, root, slow, blocking zones, and zero-health elimination.
- Stable, warning, collapsing, and void tile states driven by deterministic cannon trajectories until every stable land tile has flooded.
- At least three bot personalities implemented as data-driven utility weights.
- Iron Boots, Feather, and Spring Glove after the core gate passes.
- Four active human starting-item choices after their deterministic gates pass: four-charge three-tile Soap with owner immunity and delayed damage, four-charge two-tile Brick Bag with 20 health on each successful placement, one-charge three-second Boat, and two-charge 3.5-second Bomb dealing 80 damage to non-owners.
- Reproducible developer replay fixtures containing normalized settings, seed, human commands, versions, checkpoints, and a final state hash.
- A provider-neutral static build playable over HTTPS without an application backend.

## Failure and Recovery

- Invalid settings and replay data fail before a round begins; malformed values are never silently coerced into valid actions.
- Duplicate commands for one actor and tick are a development error. A missing command is neutral input.
- An unsupported replay format or simulation version is rejected instead of guessed or migrated.
- Falling becomes irreversible after the support grace window. Restart creates a new world rather than mutating the completed round.
- Fatal UI errors may show copyable non-secret build, seed, tick, and state-hash metadata. They are not uploaded automatically.

## Success Gates

- At least 80% of observed first-time players move within five seconds without opening instructions.
- At least 60% use or correctly explain dodge by the end of their second round.
- At least 90% of observed deaths can be explained by the player immediately afterward.
- At least 60% restart within five seconds of their first defeat, and at least half play three rounds.
- The fixed 70-participant build targets 60 rendered frames per second on the named desktop baseline and must preserve 60 authoritative simulation ticks per requested second without backlog.

These are pre-submission quality gates, not population-level market claims.

Human evidence for these gates must follow [04-playtest-protocol.md](04-playtest-protocol.md). A
developer check or five-person directional batch may identify problems but cannot claim the
pre-submission percentages have passed. Results from different behavior SHAs are not combined.

## Explicit Non-goals

- Online multiplayer, accounts, authentication, cloud saves, leaderboards, chat, or a database.
- Runtime LLMs, remote AI calls, advertising, analytics SDKs, or automatic error upload.
- Console or installed desktop support in the MVP.
- Progression, shops, unlocks, user-generated maps, skins, campaigns, or a mod API.
- User-facing replay upload, sharing, or long-term backward compatibility.
