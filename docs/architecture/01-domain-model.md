# Domain Model

- Status: Accepted combat, collapse, and round-result model
- Owner: Repository owner

## Identity and Time

- `RoundId`, `ActorId`, `TileId`, and integer `Tick` are stable identifiers.
- The simulation advances at exactly 60 integer ticks per game second.
- Actor arrays may be stored in any order, but externally meaningful resolution and hashes use stable identifiers.
- One actor may submit at most one `ActorCommandV1` per tick. Missing input is a neutral command.

## Round Configuration

`GameConfigV1` is normalized before world creation. It supports 4 through 60 participants, arenas from 7 through 48 columns and rows, a maximum 120-second replay horizon, slow, normal, or fast collapse, normal density, Easy/Normal/Hard bot difficulty, item policy version 2, a bounded initial item count, a participant-derived simultaneous cap, and a zero-to-thirty-second spawn interval. The same raw values normalize identically, and disabled items require zero initial and spawn values. Browser settings force 60 participants, Hard difficulty, Slow collapse, eight initial map items, one spawn every seven seconds, and a 120-second round limit; smaller counts and other values remain simulation fixtures and diagnostic inputs. Browser-local `GameSettings` owns six starting attributes totaling 20 points, two starting skills, and one starting charged item. A credited human elimination pauses the browser scheduler until one of six direct combat-trait commands is saved; inactive, Falling, Eliminated, completed, and all-traits-capped states cannot request or accept that choice.

The browser's public arena policy derives a 48×40 bound for 60 participants and presents it through a player-follow camera rather than shrinking it to the viewport. Smaller internal tiers retain bounded arena derivation for tests and controlled audits. The public island starts from exactly 60% of its rectangular bound and requires twelve separated 5–9-tile lake cuts whose combined budget cannot exceed 96 tiles. Every accepted public cut reaches its target size and preserves one connected landmass and the minimum land budget.

## Participant

A participant contains stable identity, human or scripted control ownership, an active flag, a circular body, two-slot inventory, three skill slots, six immutable starting attributes, combat resources, temporary effects, progression, offensive credit, and an action state. Inventory slots preserve their index, item definition, and nullable charge count; `null` means a permanent passive. Skill slots preserve definition, index, and ready tick. Combat owns current and maximum health and mana, shield amount and expiry, last damage and mana-spend ticks, last damage source, and stun, root, and slow expiry. Base health and mana are 100 before starting-attribute bonuses. Health begins regenerating five seconds after the latest damage; mana begins regenerating one second after the latest spend. Zero health takes the canonical direct-elimination path, while falling remains a separate support-loss path. The body owns position, velocity, facing, radius, base and effective continuous `massFactor`, and integer unsupported ticks. Effective mass is clamped to `0.85..1.65`, retaining the full Feather and Iron Boots multipliers around every valid starting build. Starting Strength, Agility, Constitution, Spirit, Balance, and Willpower divide exactly 20 points with a `0..20` per-attribute bound and linear per-point effects. Progression separately owns unspent kill-reward points, credited eliminations, compatibility skill-rank state, and bounded Power, Stability, Mobility, Reflex, Vitality, and Focus levels. Browser kill rewards expose only the six trait levels; compatibility skill ranks have no current human selection path. Power improves offensive impulse and skill damage, Stability reduces impulse and damage, Mobility improves direct speed, Reflex reduces cooldown ticks, Vitality improves health and health regeneration, and Focus improves mana and mana regeneration.

Action kinds are `Ready`, `ShoveWindup`, `ShoveActive`, `ShoveRecovery`, `DodgeActive`, `GrapplePull`, `Stumbling`, `Anchored`, `Falling`, and `Eliminated`. Action transitions are tick-bounded. Executable requests use `dodge > active item > shove`; an invalid, passive, exhausted, or anchorless slot falls through to shove without consuming a charge. `GrapplePull` is a 12-tick, input-locked self-pull with `0.965` drag; Wind Blast may replace it with Stumbling, while a Bomb in range eliminates it immediately. `Anchored` means a dodge contacted a Brick wall and snapped to its center: ordinary and offensive impulses cannot move the rider, the rider cannot shove, dodge, or use an item, movement input dismounts to an adjacent supported tile, and wall removal releases it back to ordinary support resolution. `Falling` is irreversible and later transitions to `Eliminated`.

The current tuning uses eight shove-windup ticks, four hand-active ticks, twenty recovery ticks, four dodge/evasion ticks, nine unsupported grace ticks, and twenty-four falling ticks. ShoveActive never forces forward body speed; it exposes a `0.32`-tile hand reach beyond the two body radii. A miss converts the attacker's current movement into at least thirty Stumbling ticks, scaled by speed and inverse mass. During `Ready`, `ShoveWindup`, `ShoveActive`, and `ShoveRecovery`, sampled movement directly sets the current mass-sensitive velocity and neutral input sets it to zero; acceleration remains only in external action physics. The minimum mass reaches `1.50×` neutral locomotion while maximum mass reaches `0.82×`; Dodge speed additionally multiplies by `1 / massFactor`, and Brick landing uses the same full speed as movement so collision intent cannot drift from travel. The collapsed local debug lab may override bounded movement speed, mass-speed, hand-reach, and dodge values for the next round without changing production defaults.

## Tile

A tile has an integer grid location, stable `column:row` ID, and a `Stable`, `Warning`, `Collapsing`, or `Void` lifecycle. The private collapse plan orders outer layers before inner layers and shuffles only within a layer through the named collapse stream. Future plan data is not exposed in `RenderFrameV1`. Stable, warning, and collapsing tiles support a participant; void tiles do not. Support is decided at the participant center after collision and impulse resolution, followed by an integer grace window.

## Brick Wall

A brick wall owns its tile ID, integer location, placing actor, and placement tick. At most one wall can occupy a tile. Successful proposals are committed in actor-ID order before active-item rays, so command-array order and attacker IDs cannot change same-tick shielding. A wall occupies its entire tile as a static axis-aligned obstacle. Participant circles use swept point-versus-radius-expanded bounds before broad phase and an overlap-only projection after weak body contacts. Wind Blast and hand shove use unexpanded wall ray bounds, with exact corner contact and distance ties favoring the wall. A wall is removed only when its tile becomes `Void`; the `tile-void` event precedes `brick-wall-removed` in that tick.

## Grappling Anchor

A Grappling Hook anchor is resolved from current authoritative tile and Brick state, not presentation geometry. Its 4.5-tile facing ray ignores participant bodies and selects the farthest non-Void tile before the first Brick wall, or the wall itself, subject to a 1.25-tile minimum. Warning and Collapsing tiles are eligible; Void, water-only, and out-of-arena coordinates are not. The anchor is a same-tick value rather than a persistent world entity. An accepted pull changes only the user's velocity by `0.24 / massFactor` toward the anchor, capped at `0.30`; it neither teleports nor changes support or offensive credit. Subsequent normal wall and body collision remains authoritative and may transfer motion without creating Hook credit.

## Bomb

A bomb owns its placing actor, snapped tile-center position, placement tick, and detonation tick. It is non-solid and does not move. `(ownerActorId, placedTick)` is its stable identity because one actor can issue at most one command per tick. Bombs are hashed in detonation-tick then actor-ID order and remain after owner elimination or tile flooding. Due bombs leave the frame only when their 300-tick fuse resolves; another explosion cannot shorten that fuse. Detonation directly eliminates every non-Falling opponent whose center lies inside the three-tile radius. The owner instead receives distance-, mass-, and Stability-sensitive launch plus 42 Stumbling ticks; nearby water can still eliminate the owner through ordinary support rules. Brick walls and Dodge do not protect opponents. Deterministic actor ordering and the shared direct-elimination helper preserve events, inventory cleanup, and credit.

## Pirate Artillery

World construction creates eight immutable pirate-ship positions 1.4 tiles beyond the arena bounds. Flood fill separates boundary-connected outer ocean from enclosed lakes. Collapse depth begins only at land touching that outer ocean, so lakes do not become independent inward artillery fronts. Each six-tile collapse wave selects one currently exposed outer-coast tile with an unobstructed sampled ocean approach, assigns the nearest clear ship, and creates one `CannonShotState`. A ship cannot overlap its own flights and remains unavailable for 120 ticks after impact. Cannon launch targets a 210-tick flight, the orange warning begins during flight, the red danger stage follows, and impact shares the authoritative `Void` tick. Ship ammunition is derived from sorted launch ticks rather than mutable state, and active cannon shots are presentation facts rather than collision entities.

The final cannon impact leaves the connected protected core intact. Sixty ticks later the same ships enter an unlimited rock phase. `RockShotState` snapshots target position at launch, flies for ninety ticks, and directly eliminates every standing body within `0.72` tiles of impact. Launch intervals shrink from ninety to sixty-six to forty-eight ticks as standing count crosses eight and four. Target choice consumes only the named `artillery` random stream; active shots, the next launch tick, and the next shot ID enter the world hash.

## Treasure Delivery

The item system derives one `TreasureShipState` from the immutable arena bounds and current tick. Its 2,400-tick elliptical orbit stays outside the island and requires no mutable movement state. A due replacement keeps the existing 3:2:1 spawn-band draw, restricts the chosen band to the sixteen candidates nearest the current ship position, reserves the next item ID, and creates one `GiftDeliveryState` with a 72-tick flight. The ship never owns more than one active gift. Landing creates the `ItemState` only when the target remains Stable, unblocked, and under the area-adjusted cap; otherwise the reserved delivery expires. Active deliveries and the delivery cursor are canonical hashed state. Initial items remain ordinary pre-delivered `ItemState` values so the opening loadout contest starts immediately.

## Round Result

`RoundStateV1` is `Active` or `Completed`. A completed result records exactly one of `last-standing`, `no-survivors`, or `time-limit`, an optional winner, and the completion tick. The world becomes sealed after completion and rejects additional steps. Falling is already irreversible, so one grounded participant may win while the others are still completing their fall animation. A hard time limit with multiple standing participants does not invent a winner.

## Commands, Frames, and Events

- `ActorCommandV1` contains tick, actor ID, normalized movement, shove/dodge edge flags, an optional inventory slot `0|1`, and an optional stat-spend request.
- `RenderFrameV1` is an immutable presentation snapshot with current and previous positions, facing, mass, inventory slots, effects, Spring Glove telegraph, map items, active treasure gifts, treasure-ship position, brick walls, armed bombs, pirate ships, active cannon and rock shots, action, tiles, tick, and state hash.
- `SimulationEventV1` is a versioned, ordered fact stream for one-time presentation and diagnostics. Events do not drive authoritative physics.

Human input and bots must use the same command path. A bot cannot directly set position, velocity, cooldown, action state, or tile state.

## Randomness

The versioned XorShift32 generator is seeded from a master seed. Named streams isolate `arena`, `collapse`, `items`, `tie-break`, `artillery`, `bot-personality:<ActorId>`, and `bot-jitter:<ActorId>`. Consuming one stream cannot change another stream's sequence.

Randomness may select arena variants, content placement, bot personality data, and small decision jitter. It cannot randomly decide collision outcomes, shove success, dodge success, support, or mass response.

## Replay

`ReplayFixtureV2` stores format, product, simulation, and content versions; build ID; normalized config; master seed; human actor ID; required base mass and starting loadout; end tick; strictly increasing human commands; ordered hash checkpoints; and a final hash.

The current format accepts UTF-8 JSON up to 5 MiB and 7,200 ticks. Unknown replay majors, incompatible simulation versions, malformed booleans or numbers, commands for bots, duplicate or unordered ticks, range violations, and hash mismatches are errors. Compatibility is never guessed.
