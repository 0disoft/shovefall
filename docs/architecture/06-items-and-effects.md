# Items and Effects

- Status: Implemented gray-box content contract
- Owner: Simulation and content boundaries

## Definitions

`src/content/items.ts` is the declarative source for eight item primitives: Iron Boots, Feather, Spring Glove, Wind Blast, Brick Bag, Boat, Bomb, and Soap. Every item owns its stable ID, user-visible label, presentation keys, loadout class, targeting metadata, charges, and effect numbers. `src/content/skills.ts` separately owns the eight combat skills: Force Palm, Blink Step, Arc Bolt, Chain Bind, Meteor Mark, Frost Field, Tidal Charge, and Aegis. `src/content/built-in-actions.ts` owns the shared Grappling Hook. Skills do not call item implementations or consume inventory charges; items remain charged or passive inventory tools, skills remain renewable mana-backed actions, and the Hook remains an always-equipped action.

The public inventory exposes one selected active item in slot `D`. Random map pickups use charged active definitions and replace the depleted public slot. Iron Boots, Feather, and Spring Glove remain supported as internal or historical effect primitives but are not public starting cards or random public drops.

Wind Blast starts with two charges when selected as an item. `D`, its gamepad binding, or the item button requests that slot. Arc Bolt is the distinct renewable ranged skill: it spends mana, starts its own cooldown, deals health damage, and applies `0.30` knockback without sharing Wind Blast charges or item resolution. Both attacks first preserve exact ray-circle hits, then admit a target only inside the same bounded forward cone with a minimum aim dot of `0.94`. The assisted direction points at that target, and wall/tree occlusion is tested along that actual path before deterministic nearest-entry and actor-ID tie-breaking. Launched bodies can transfer motion through swept weak contacts, and the strongest same-tick offensive impulse owns elimination credit.

Brick Bag starts with four charges and targets a tile within two world tiles. A successful proposal requires a non-Void tile with no existing wall, tree, map item, Soap patch, or collidable participant. Competing proposals resolve by lower actor ID. Each accepted wall consumes one charge and heals its active owner by 20 up to maximum health; invalid and losing proposals heal nothing. All accepted walls commit before Wind Blast, block ray and hand-shove line of sight, stop swept participant motion without reflection, and disappear when their supporting tile becomes Void. A Dodge path that reaches a wall snaps to its center and enters `Anchored`; the rider cannot be displaced or attack and leaves on movement input or wall loss.

Boat starts with one charge and adds a refreshable effect ending exactly 180 ticks after activation. Normal land support still wins; on water, Boat supports only when the participant center remains inside a tile ID that belongs to the generated arena. While aboard, the participant cannot use skills or items and regenerates neither health nor mana. Boat never supports coordinates beyond the arena, changes no combat or mass value, and cannot reverse Falling.

Bomb starts with two charges and snaps to a valid selected tile. Same-tile proposals resolve by actor ID without charging losers. The exact 210-tick fuse survives flooding and owner elimination, then deals 80 damage to every collidable non-owner participant inside its three-tile radius. The owner remains subject to outward launch and Stumbling but skips self-damage. The impulse uses distance falloff, mass and Stability response, and a bounded cap. Bombs do not collide, move, or chain-detonate.

Soap starts with four charges and places a one-use patch on a selected tile within three world tiles. The target must be a non-Void tile without a Brick wall, tree, armed Bomb, Soap patch, map item, or collidable body; failed and losing actor-ID proposals spend nothing. After movement and static/body contacts, the lowest-ID non-owner body whose center enters the patch consumes it, keeps its velocity direction or falls back to facing, clamps speed to `0.105..0.42`, and Stumbles for 60 ticks. A victim receives the Soap owner's ordinary offensive credit. Soap is non-solid, disappears with a Void transition, and blocks later Brick or Bomb placement on its tile.

Grappling Hook is a built-in reusable `E` action with a ten-second cooldown. It casts a deterministic 4.5-tile ray along the user's locked facing. Bodies are ignored. The hook selects the farthest current non-Void tile anchor before the first Brick wall, or that Brick wall when it is at least 1.25 tiles away. Warning and Collapsing tiles remain valid until their transition to Void; water, Void tiles, coordinates outside the generated arena, and Falling actors are invalid. A missing or too-close anchor spends no cooldown. Same-tick Brick proposals commit before hook acquisition, so command-array order cannot change the ray.

An accepted hook moves only its user toward the static anchor: it changes velocity by `0.24 / massFactor`, clamps pull speed to `0.30`, and enters `GrapplePull` for exactly 12 ticks with `0.965` drag. It never teleports, grants support, resets unsupported ticks, targets or directly pulls another body, or creates offensive credit. Existing swept body contact can still transfer motion. Wind Blast can override `GrapplePull` with Stumbling and credit; a same-tick hostile Bomb in range can eliminate the user. Boat support and expiry remain independent. The human chooses one active starting item and two reusable skills. Each bot receives one deterministically balanced active item.

## Skill Combat Lifecycle

Every active participant begins with 100 health and 100 mana before starting-attribute bonuses. A successful skill commit checks its selected slot, ready tick, control restrictions, and mana before spending mana and writing the cooldown. Invalid casts spend neither. Force Palm deals 18 damage at 1.7 tiles, assists within roughly twenty degrees, and stuns for two seconds. Chain Bind assists within roughly fifteen degrees, deals 12 damage, and roots for 1.2 seconds. Blink Step costs 20 mana. Arc Bolt costs 32 mana, has a six-second cooldown, reaches 3.5 tiles, and applies `0.30` knockback. Frost Field deals 5 damage while preserving its five-second 25% slow. Tidal Charge has a five-second cooldown. Aegis costs 45 mana, has a twelve-second cooldown, and for five seconds absorbs 24 damage while reducing incoming control duration by 30%.

Damage first consumes active shield and then Stability-reduced health. The latest valid hostile damage source owns a zero-health elimination credit. Ordinary hand shove also deals bounded health damage, so repeated contact has a visible combat consequence without replacing positional elimination. Every nonzero skill impulse also assigns a definition-owned Stumbling duration before movement intent runs; this preserves Arc Bolt, Force Palm, Meteor Mark, and Tidal Charge displacement instead of letting Ready movement overwrite it. Anchored bodies ignore those impulses. Health regeneration begins only after its no-damage delay; mana regeneration begins after its shorter no-spend delay. Vitality and Focus raise capacities and their matching regeneration. Skill ranks reduce mana and cooldown while raising relevant damage, shield, impulse, and control duration.

The public `E` action is a built-in Grappling Hook rather than an inventory item. Every participant owns one cooldown, and a valid cast pulls only its user toward a current land, tree, or Brick anchor. Invalid casts spend no cooldown. Spring Glove is consumed by the next successful Hook and multiplies that cast's range and pull speed. Bomb owners remain subject to blast impulse but skip self-damage; Soap patches ignore their owner and remain armed for another participant.

## Deterministic Item Lifecycle

The `items` random stream alone chooses placement and definition. Initial items use `ceil(participants × 0.33)` unless a bounded setting overrides it and are treated as supplies delivered before play. The absolute simultaneous cap is `ceil(participants × 0.5)` and shrinks with the remaining stable-tile ratio. When the cap shrinks, the oldest IDs are retained. A due replacement advances its schedule even when no valid tile exists, preventing a per-tick retry storm.

New deliveries require stable tiles, participant clearance, and item clearance. Candidate selection classifies the current stable footprint, including newly exposed collapse frontiers, then uses integer weights 3 for its outer ring, 2 for its second ring, and 1 for its interior. Within the selected band, the random draw is restricted to the sixteen candidates nearest the treasure ship's current orbital position, preventing a gift from crossing most of the island. One gift flies for 72 ticks and the ship cannot launch another while it remains airborne. The item is reserved but not collectible during flight; it materializes only if its target is still stable, unblocked, and below the current cap on impact. Failed landings disappear without a retry. Existing items may remain on warning or collapsing tiles as visible risk, but void-tile items are removed. Pickup runs before collapse transitions in the same tick. Contestants are ordered by distance, and exact ties use the separate seeded tie-break stream rather than human identity.

Wind Blast, Brick Bag, Boat, Bomb, and Soap are the public random map drops. Wind Blast reaches 3.5 tiles. Hard bots consider Brick Bag cover under nearby threat, edge pressure, or low-health pressure, but only at useful non-point-blank target distances. Timed duplicate effects refresh. Effective mass is recomputed from immutable base mass and unique active modifier IDs, then clamped to the global range. Effects expiring at tick `T` are removed before movement and support at `T`; Boat therefore supports activation ticks `T..T+179` and ordinary unsupported grace begins at `T+180`. Falling clears temporary effects but does not rewrite inventory. Falling and eliminated participants cannot acquire map items.

## Presentation and AI

`RenderFrameV1` exposes immutable skill slots and zones, health, mana, shield, control expiries, inventory slots, item instances, treasure-ship position and gift deliveries, active effects, armed bombs, Soap patches, effective mass, action state, and combat telegraphs. Combat state, skill ready ticks, selected-skill ranks, inventory slots, effects, action state, active zones, gift deliveries and cursors, canonical Bomb state, and canonical Soap state enter the deterministic state hash. PixiJS draws nearby health and mana bars, the orbiting gold-and-teal treasure ship, arcing gift boxes and landing rings, skill cast and hit feedback, shield feedback, and zone boundaries without mutating the world. The DOM reports `Q/W/E` skill and Hook states, the `D` item charge state, current resources, remaining timed effects, and effective stat bonuses. `action-targeting.ts` derives aim previews from content definitions, while `action-hud.ts` derives button state and copy without DOM access. Hard bots use the same skill, resource, physics, and item command fields after delayed perception and bounded utility checks. Their navigation cache owns Stable terrain depth separately from current tree and Brick occupancy; clear segments stay direct, blocked segments use a bounded deterministic detour, and stalled movement adds persistent side-biased crowd steering. Target commitment suppresses near-tie oscillation. Offensive skills require contextual range and obstacle visibility before utility scoring. Before Dodge or Blink Step, bots sample the full mass- and Agility-adjusted route and require Stable unblocked support through landing; unsafe emergency directions fall back to routed walking or Aegis. They cannot see future deliveries, private collapse plans, or Soap patches.
