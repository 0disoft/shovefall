# Items and Effects

- Status: Implemented gray-box content contract
- Owner: Simulation and content boundaries

## Definitions

`src/content/items.ts` is the declarative source for seven item primitives. Soap, Brick Bag, Boat, and Bomb are the four active public items. Iron Boots, Feather, and Spring Glove remain internal or historical effect primitives. Every item owns its stable ID, user-visible label, presentation keys, loadout class, targeting metadata, charges, and effect numbers. `src/content/skills.ts` separately owns the six combat skills: Blink Step, Arc Bolt, Chain Bind, Meteor Mark, Frost Field, and Aegis. `src/content/built-in-actions.ts` owns the shared Grappling Hook. Skills do not call item implementations or consume inventory charges; items remain charged or passive inventory tools, skills remain renewable mana-backed actions, and the Hook remains an always-equipped action.

The public inventory exposes one selected active item in slot `D`. Random map pickups use charged active definitions and replace the depleted public slot. Iron Boots, Feather, and Spring Glove remain supported as internal or historical effect primitives but are not public starting cards or random public drops.

Soap starts with four charges and targets a stable, unoccupied tile within three world tiles. The owner never triggers the patch. The first other active participant to enter it consumes the patch, slides in its entry direction for 120 ticks, receives 30 definition-owned ordinary damage when that slide ends, and is then stunned for 120 ticks. The damage passes through the normal shield and mitigation pipeline and credits the installer.

Brick Bag starts with three charges and targets a tile within two world tiles. A successful proposal requires a non-Void tile with no existing wall, Soap patch, tree, armed Bomb, map item, or collidable participant. Competing proposals resolve by lower actor ID. Each accepted wall consumes one charge and heals its active owner by 7 up to maximum health; invalid and losing proposals heal nothing. All accepted walls stop swept participant motion without reflection and disappear when their supporting tile becomes Void. A Dodge path that reaches a wall snaps to its center and enters `Anchored`; the rider cannot be displaced or attack and leaves on movement input or wall loss.

Boat starts with one charge and adds a refreshable effect ending exactly 150 ticks after activation. Normal land support still wins; on water, Boat supports only when the participant center remains inside a tile ID that belongs to the generated arena. While aboard, the participant cannot use skills or items and regenerates neither health nor mana. Boat never supports coordinates beyond the arena, changes no combat or mass value, and cannot reverse Falling.

Bomb starts with two charges and snaps to a valid selected tile. Same-tile proposals resolve by actor ID without charging losers. The exact 195-tick fuse survives flooding and owner elimination, then deals 60 damage inside its three-tile radius. The installer receives 25% of that damage, or 15 before mitigation, and remains subject to outward launch and Stumbling. The impulse uses distance falloff, mass and Stability response, and a bounded cap. Bombs do not collide, move, or chain-detonate.

Grappling Hook is a built-in reusable `E` action with a 10.5-second cooldown. It casts a deterministic 4.5-tile ray along the user's locked facing. Bodies and ordinary ground are ignored; only the first tree or Brick wall at least 1.25 tiles away is a valid anchor. A missing or too-close obstacle spends no cooldown. Same-tick Brick proposals commit before hook acquisition, so command-array order cannot change the ray.

An accepted hook moves only its user toward the obstacle anchor. It accelerates by `0.24 / massFactor` toward a `0.30` speed cap on every pull tick and enters `GrapplePull` for exactly 16 ticks. Shared swept obstacle collision stops the body immediately in front of the anchor, so the hook cannot tunnel through a tree or wall. It never teleports, grants support, resets unsupported ticks, targets or directly pulls another body, or creates offensive credit. Existing swept body contact can still transfer motion. A same-tick hostile Bomb in range can eliminate the user. Boat support and expiry remain independent. The human chooses one active starting item and two reusable skills. Each bot receives one deterministically balanced active item.

## Skill Combat Lifecycle

Every active participant begins with 100 health and 30 current mana before starting-attribute capacity bonuses. A successful skill commit checks its selected slot, ready tick, control restrictions, and mana before spending mana and writing the cooldown. Invalid casts spend neither. Chain Bind costs 30 mana, assists within roughly twenty-three degrees, deals 20 damage, roots for one second, and transfers up to 10 current mana from a hit target to its living caster. Blink Step costs 20 mana, travels up to four tiles, and evades attacks for two seconds. Arc Bolt costs 30 mana, has a five-second cooldown, reaches 3.5 tiles, deals 25 damage, and applies definition-owned knockback plus a one-second stumble. Meteor Mark costs 36 mana, has an eight-second cooldown, and covers a three-tile radius after its delay. Frost Field costs 38 mana, has a ten-second cooldown, and creates a four-second field that deals 4 damage per second, slows by 20%, and heals its caster for 10% of actual damage. Aegis costs 40 mana, has a twelve-second cooldown, and for four seconds absorbs 22 damage while reducing incoming control duration by 30%.

Damage first consumes active shield and then Stability-reduced health. The latest valid hostile damage source owns a zero-health elimination credit. Every nonzero skill impulse also assigns a definition-owned Stumbling duration before movement intent runs; this preserves Arc Bolt and Meteor Mark displacement instead of letting Ready movement overwrite it. Anchored bodies ignore those impulses. Health regeneration begins only after its no-damage delay; mana regeneration begins after its shorter no-spend delay. Vitality and Focus raise capacities and their matching regeneration. Skill ranks reduce mana and cooldown while raising relevant damage, shield, impulse, and control duration.

The public `E` action is a built-in Grappling Hook rather than an inventory item. Every participant owns one cooldown, and a valid cast pulls only its user toward a tree or Brick anchor. Ordinary ground is never an anchor, and invalid casts spend no cooldown. Spring Glove is consumed by the next successful Hook and multiplies that cast's range and pull speed. Bomb owners remain subject to blast impulse and take the definition-owned fraction of their own blast damage.

## Deterministic Item Lifecycle

The `items` random stream alone chooses placement and definition. Initial items use `ceil(participants × 0.33)` unless a bounded setting overrides it and are treated as supplies delivered before play. The absolute simultaneous cap is `ceil(participants × 0.5)` and shrinks with the remaining stable-tile ratio. When the cap shrinks, the oldest IDs are retained. A due replacement advances its schedule even when no valid tile exists, preventing a per-tick retry storm.

New deliveries require stable tiles, participant clearance, item clearance, and a launch-time distance of three through seven complete stable tiles from the nearest water boundary. Two treasure ships stay half an orbit apart and alternate launch opportunities at half the configured pair interval, giving the public seven-second setting an average 3.5-second launch cadence. The random draw is restricted to the sixteen eligible candidates nearest the firing ship, preventing a gift from crossing most of the island. One gift flies for 72 ticks and each ship cannot launch another while its own gift remains airborne. The item is reserved but not collectible during flight; it materializes only if its target is still stable, unblocked, and below the current cap on impact. Failed landings disappear without a retry. Existing items may remain on warning or collapsing tiles as visible risk, but void-tile items are removed. Pickup runs before collapse transitions in the same tick. Contestants are ordered by distance, and exact ties use the separate seeded tie-break stream rather than human identity.

Soap, Brick Bag, Boat, and Bomb are the four active public starting choices and random map drops. Hard bots consider Brick Bag cover under nearby threat, edge pressure, or low-health pressure, and consider Soap when an opponent creates nearby pressure. Timed duplicate effects refresh. Effective mass is recomputed from immutable base mass and unique active modifier IDs, then clamped to the global range. Effects expiring at tick `T` are removed before movement and support at `T`; Boat therefore supports activation ticks `T..T+149` and ordinary unsupported grace begins at `T+150`. Falling clears temporary effects but does not rewrite inventory. Falling and eliminated participants cannot acquire map items.

## Presentation and AI

`RenderFrameV1` exposes immutable skill slots and zones, health, mana, shield, control expiries, inventory slots, item instances, treasure-ship position and gift deliveries, active effects, armed bombs, effective mass, action state, and combat telegraphs. Combat state, skill ready ticks, selected-skill ranks, inventory slots, effects, action state, active zones, gift deliveries and cursors, and canonical Bomb state enter the deterministic state hash. PixiJS draws nearby health and mana bars, the orbiting gold-and-teal treasure ship, arcing gift boxes and landing rings, skill cast and hit feedback, shield feedback, and zone boundaries without mutating the world. The DOM reports `Q/W/E` skill and Hook states, the `D` item charge state, current resources, remaining timed effects, and effective stat bonuses. `action-targeting.ts` derives aim previews from content definitions, while `action-hud.ts` derives button state and copy without DOM access. Hard bots use the same skill, resource, physics, and item command fields after delayed perception and bounded utility checks. Their navigation cache owns Stable terrain depth separately from current tree and Brick occupancy; clear segments stay direct, blocked segments use a bounded deterministic detour, and stalled movement adds persistent side-biased crowd steering. Target commitment suppresses near-tie oscillation. Offensive skills require contextual range and obstacle visibility before utility scoring. Before Dodge or Blink Step, bots sample the full mass- and Agility-adjusted route and require Stable unblocked support through landing; unsafe emergency directions fall back to routed walking or Aegis. They cannot see future deliveries or private collapse plans.

Boat has one explicit support contract. It cannot be consumed on supported land. A charged Boat is automatically consumed when its owner enters generated-arena sea or lake water, including involuntary knockback. The three-second effect provides water support but blocks skills, Grappling Hook, other items, health regeneration, and mana regeneration. Positions outside the generated arena remain unsupported and cannot trigger automatic boarding.
